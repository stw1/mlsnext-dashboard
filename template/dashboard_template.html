How the predictions work

Each team gets an attack and a defense rating, fit to every completed game with a Poisson goals model (the standard approach for soccer score prediction). Expected goals for the home side = league average × home edge × home attack × away defense weakness, and the same for the away side. Ratings are deliberately shrunk toward average because only ~3 games per team have been played. Early-season numbers will move a lot.

From the two expected-goal numbers the model works out the chance of every scoreline (0–0, 1–0, 2–1, …), which gives win/draw/loss chances and the single most likely score. "Pick" is the most likely outcome; the margin is the expected goal difference rounded. The season projection plays out every remaining fixture 10,000 times using those chances.

Not included: roster changes, injuries, travel, weather, MLS NEXT Flex or out-of-conference games, and the fact that U14 results are noisy. Treat these as a guide, not a guarantee.

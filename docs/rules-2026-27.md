# MLS NEXT Homegrown Division rules used by the dashboard (2026-27)

Source: *MLS NEXT Allstate Homegrown Division Rules & Regulations 2026-27* (39 pages, dated July 13, 2026):
https://images.mlssoccer.com/image/upload/v1783990779/assets/MLS_NEXT_HD_Rules_and_Regulations_2026-27_Final_Web_Version_hwmkha.pdf

This is a summary of the parts that affect the site, with section numbers, and how the site follows each one.
Last checked against the rules and the live MLS standings feed on 2026-10-02.

## Standings (XIII.k)

- Win 3, tie 1, loss 0. **A forfeit is a 0–3 loss.**
- MLS posts official standings for **U15–U19 only**. The feed has no official values for U13/U14, so the
  site works those tables out from the results using the same rules, and says so under the table.
- Showcases (regional and winter) are part of the regular season and count in the standings (II.bb, II.oo).
  League games between teams of different brackets are kept and counted in each team's own table (see below).

## Tiebreakers (XIII.l, XIII.m)

Teams level on **points per match** are separated in this order:

1. Head-to-head, **only for a tie of exactly two teams**. In a three-way tie the process never goes back to
   head-to-head, even once only two teams remain.
2. Wins per match
3. Goal difference per match
4. Goals for per match
5. Away goal difference per match
6. Away goals for per match
7. Home goal difference per match
8. Home goals for per match
9. Fewest disciplinary points (yellow 1, red 3, staff/player ejection 5)
10. Coin toss (two teams) or drawing lots (three or more)

Wildcard tiebreakers work the same way as conference tiebreakers.

How the site follows this:

- Steps 5–8 are worked out **per away match / per home match**. Dividing by all matches gave a different order
  from MLS's in 2 of 44 conferences. Per away/home match, or plain totals, both match all 44.
- Steps 9–10 aren't in the data, so teams level on steps 1–8 keep **MLS's own published position**
  (`DATA.opos`, U15–U19).
- `scripts/check_official.py` repeats this comparison after every auto-update (see CLAUDE.md).
- Result on 2026-10-02: the site matches MLS's records for all 568 U15–U19 teams (games played, W-D-L, goals),
  and MLS's order in all 44 U15–U19 conferences.

## Postseason (II.w, II.jj, XIII.b)

- The Post-Season is the **MLS NEXT Cup**, for qualifying teams from **U15–U19** (2027 dates: June 5–13).
  It grows from 32 to 48 teams per age group in 2026-27. Teams qualify through league position, the Pro Player
  Pathway or MLS NEXT Flex, but **the split of spots isn't published yet**. The site shows a "Top 4" line,
  labelled as not official.
- U13/U14: in 2025-26 each conference sent teams to Championship and Premier brackets (UpNext Analytics, March
  2026). The site uses those as `CUP_EST` lines marked "(est.)" until MLS publishes the 2026-27 allocation.
- Teams that don't play their full schedule can be excluded from the Post-Season (XIII.i).

## Match rules that matter for data

- A match is complete once it reaches minute 50 (U13), 60 (U14) or 75 (U15–U19). Otherwise MLS decides whether
  to replay it, forfeit it or cancel it (XIII.h).
- League ties stand (1 point each). **Flex** is a separate competition: drawn games go to a
  penalty shootout. Its points (3 for a win, 2 for a shootout win, 1 for a shootout loss) come from MLS's Flex
  tables, not from this rulebook.

## Data quirks found in the 2026-27 feed (and how the build handles them)

- **Future-dated "completed" games.** A few games are marked completed, with a score, but dated after the feed's
  sync time. MLS's standings ignore them, so `refresh.py` treats them as not played yet.
- **Teams missing from the standings feed.** FC Bay Area Surf (U15 Northwest) and New England Revolution (U14
  Northeast) play full league schedules but aren't in the standings feed. MLS still counts their opponents'
  games against them. The build adds each team to its opponents' conference, so its games and table row show.
- **Cross-age league games.** San Diego FC U16 plays the U17 West Pro Player Pathway schedule. MLS counts each
  game in each team's own table (SD in U16, the opponent in U17), and so does the site. Those games stay out of
  the ratings and Who beat who, and the opponent is labelled with its age (for example "LA Galaxy U17").
